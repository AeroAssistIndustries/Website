How do you handle patient privacy?

Posts never include patient information without written permission, and review replies never confirm that someone is a patient or mention treatment. You approve content before it goes live.

Can you write about treatments?

I write clear, friendly explanations based on information you provide or approve. Clinical claims always come from you.

Do you work with group practices?

Yes. Extra locations can be added to any plan, quoted after the audit.
