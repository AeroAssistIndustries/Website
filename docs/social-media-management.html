Questions

Good to know.

More answers on the FAQ page.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Many great local accounts never show the owner. I'll plan posts around your products, space, team and customers (with their permission). If you are comfortable on camera, short videos of you usually perform well.

Who takes the photos and videos?

You or your team take quick phone clips using my simple shot list, or I use your existing photos and approved stock images. On-site photo shoots can be added as a one-time project.

Will I approve posts before they go out?

Yes. You see each month's calendar before anything is scheduled, and you can change or remove any post.

How soon will I see results?

Most accounts look active and consistent in the first month. Growth in followers, messages and visits usually builds over two to three months of steady posting.

Is ad spend included?

No. On the Pro plan I manage boosted posts and simple ads, and the ad budget is paid directly to Meta, so you always see exactly what's spent.
