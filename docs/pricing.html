Billing questions

Good to know.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans run month to month with 30 days' notice to stop. The only exception is the Website Growth and Pro plans, which include a new site with no build fee in exchange for a 12-month plan.

Which plan should I start with?

Most local businesses start with Local SEO or Reputation, because that's where nearby customers find and judge you first. Your free audit ends with a clear recommendation and an exact monthly price.

How do combo savings work?

Two services save 10%, three save 15%, and all four save 20%, whether you pick a ready-made combo or build your own with different levels. Combo prices are rounded down to the nearest $5. Founding clients can choose 15% off for their first 6 months instead; the discounts don't stack.

Is ad spend included?

No. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google or Meta from your own account. My fee covers managing the ads.

Who owns my accounts and content?

You do. I work inside accounts in your name, and every post, photo and page I create is yours to keep.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for a year upfront and you get 12 months for the price of 10, on any plan, combo or the All-in-One packages. Yearly savings stack with combo savings. The founding client rate is for monthly plans. Yearly plans renew each year unless you cancel before the renewal date.

What's the All-in-One Starter?

All four services in one lighter plan for $595 a month: social media on one platform, your Google profile set up and posted to, replies to your reviews, and website hosting and care. It's the lowest price for everything, and you can move up to a Complete plan any month.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Move up, down or add a service at the start of any month.

How do I pay?

You get a monthly invoice you can pay online by card or bank transfer (ACH).
