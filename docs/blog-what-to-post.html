What to post when you have nothing to post: 30 ideas

By Karla · November 18, 2026 · 2 min read

In this post
  1. Behind the scenes
  2. People
  3. Products and services
  4. Helpful tips
  5. Community and moments
  6. Turn the list into a plan

Every business owner hits the blank screen. The fix isn't more inspiration, it's a list. Here are thirty ideas, grouped into five themes, so you can plan a whole month in one sitting.

Behind the scenes

  1. Opening up in the morning: lights on, first coffee, the quiet before the rush.
  2. A time-lapse of something being made, cleaned or set up.
  3. Your workspace, tools or kitchen, and why it's set up that way.
  4. A delivery arriving and what's in it.
  5. The prep nobody sees before a busy weekend.
  6. A mistake that happened, and how you fixed it.

People

  1. Meet a team member: their role, their favorite thing on the menu or in the shop.
  2. Your own story: why you started and what keeps you going.
  3. A regular customer, with their permission and in their words.
  4. A supplier or local partner you love working with.
  5. A thank-you to your neighbors on the street.
  6. Answers to "what's it like to work here?"

Products and services

  1. Your best-seller, and why people come back for it.
  2. Something new, or back for the season.
  3. A before and after.
  4. How to choose between two options you offer.
  5. A detail close-up: texture, color, craftsmanship.
  6. What's included in a service, step by step.

Helpful tips

  1. A quick how-to related to what you do.
  2. The question customers ask most, answered.
  3. A myth in your industry, gently corrected.
  4. How to care for something they bought from you.
  5. Three things to check before booking or buying.
  6. A seasonal tip for this time of year.

Community and moments

  1. A local event you're part of or going to.
  2. Your neighborhood: a view, a landmark, a favorite spot nearby.
  3. A milestone: an anniversary, a number of customers served, a new hire.
  4. A review you're proud of, with a short thank-you.
  5. Holiday hours and plans, posted early.
  6. A simple question for your followers to answer in the comments.

Turn the list into a plan

Pick one theme for each day you post and rotate through them. Three posts a week from this list covers ten weeks. Batch your photos on a quiet afternoon, write short captions in your own voice, and always tell people what to do next: call, book, visit or message.
