Hello from Digital Mondo

By Karla · November 14, 2026 · 2 min read

In this post
  1. Why a one-person agency
  2. A worldly eye for local businesses
  3. What I help with
  4. What you'll find on this blog

Welcome to the Digital Mondo blog. This is where I'll share what I'm learning about helping local businesses get found, chosen and remembered, in plain English and without the jargon.

Why a one-person agency

Most small business owners I talk to have tried marketing help before. Often it went the same way: a big promise, a long contract, a different account manager every few months and a monthly report full of numbers nobody explained.

Digital Mondo is built the other way around. You work with me, the same person every month. I learn your business, your customers and the way you like to talk to them. Plans are month to month, because I'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

A worldly eye for local businesses

I've traveled widely and seen how people in different places discover a café, book a salon or choose a hotel. Some plan everything on Google Maps. Some ask friends in a group chat. Some scroll short videos until something looks good enough to visit. More and more ask an AI assistant.

That's the idea behind our tagline, your neighborhood, seen by the world. Your next customer might live two streets away, or they might be landing at the airport tonight. Either way, they should find you, like what they see and know exactly how to reach you.

What I help with

  • Social media management: regular posts, Reels and replies that sound like you.
  • Local SEO: your Google Business Profile, Maps and the details AI search tools rely on.
  • Reputation management: more genuine reviews, and a thoughtful reply to every one.
  • Websites: fast, simple sites that turn visitors into calls and bookings.

You can see what each plan includes on the pricing page, or start with a free marketing audit to see where you stand today.

What you'll find on this blog

Short, practical posts you can act on the same day: what to post this week, how to answer a tricky review, which Google settings matter, and what's changing in how people search. For longer deep dives, there's the guides library, and the free tools are there whenever you need them.

If there's something you'd like me to write about, tell me. Chances are other business owners are wondering the same thing.
