How I create content

From idea to posted,
in four steps.

You stay in control of what goes out. I handle the planning, design, writing and scheduling.

01

Brand kickoff

I learn your goals, customers, offers and voice, and gather your logo, colors and best photos.

02

Monthly calendar

You get a calendar of every post for the month, built around your promotions, seasons and local events.

03

Design & approve

I design the posts and write captions. You approve or request changes before anything is published.

04
