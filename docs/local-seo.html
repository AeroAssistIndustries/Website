Questions

Good to know.

More answers on the FAQ page.

How long does local SEO take?

Fixing your profile and listings can make a difference within weeks. Climbing for competitive searches usually takes three to six months of steady work. Anyone who promises a number one ranking is guessing.

Can you guarantee a top spot on Google Maps?

No one honestly can. Rankings depend on your distance from the searcher, your competition and Google's own systems. What I can promise is a complete, accurate, active profile and site, which is what Google rewards.

What is AI search visibility?

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers for recommendations. On the Pro plan I check how your business appears in those answers and make sure the information they draw on, like your profile, website and listings, is clear and consistent.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Not always. Many sites just need better page titles, clearer service pages and faster loading. If yours needs more, the Website plans include a new site with no big upfront cost.

Will you change my business name or address?

Only to fix mistakes. Your name on Google should match your real-world name, and adding keywords to it breaks Google's guidelines.
