How to reply to a negative review (with templates you can copy)

By Karla · November 20, 2026 · 3 min read

In this post
  1. Before you reply
  2. The five-part reply
  3. Templates you can copy
  4. What to avoid
  5. If the review looks fake

A negative review stings, especially when you've worked hard. But your reply is read by every future customer who finds that review, so it's a chance to show how you treat people when something goes wrong.

Before you reply

Wait until you're calm, ideally within a day or two. Check what actually happened: your booking records, the receipt, your team's side. Remember that you're not writing for the reviewer alone. You're writing for the next hundred people who read it.

The five-part reply

  1. Thank them for taking the time to share feedback.
  2. Acknowledge what went wrong for them, without arguing about the details.
  3. Apologize for their experience, sincerely and briefly.
  4. Explain what you're doing about it, if there's something to share.
  5. Take it offline with a name and a direct way to reach you.

Keep it short, sign it with your name, and leave out anything private, such as booking details, health information or what they ordered. That matters most for medical, dental and wellness practices.

Templates you can copy

Slow service or a long wait

Thank you for letting us know, [name]. You're right that we kept you waiting longer than we should have, and I'm sorry. We've changed how we schedule busy times so it doesn't happen again. I'd love the chance to make it up to you. Please reach me directly at [phone or email]. [Your name], owner

The product or result wasn't right

Hi [name], thank you for the honest feedback, and I'm sorry this didn't meet your expectations. That's not the standard we aim for. Please get in touch at [phone or email] so I can understand what happened and put it right. [Your name]

A staff member was rude

[Name], thank you for telling us. Everyone who walks in should feel welcome, and I'm sorry you didn't. I've shared your feedback with the team and we're addressing it. If you're willing, I'd like to hear more at [phone or email]. [Your name], owner

You can't find them in your records

Hi [name], thank you for your feedback. I take every review seriously, but I wasn't able to find your visit in our records. Could you contact me at [phone or email] so I can look into it properly? [Your name]

What to avoid

  • Arguing, blaming the customer or correcting them in public.
  • Copying the same reply onto every review. People notice.
  • Offering a refund or freebie in exchange for changing or removing the review.
  • Sharing anything private about the customer or their visit.

If the review looks fake

If a review breaks Google's policies, for example it's spam, off-topic, from a competitor or from someone who was never a customer, you can report it from your Business Profile. Reply politely anyway in the meantime, because the review stays visible while Google looks into it.
