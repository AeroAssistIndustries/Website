Questions

Good to know.

More answers on the FAQ page.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain and your content are yours. When a plan ends, I hand over the logins I set up for you and the files you have paid for.

Why is there no upfront fee?

On the Growth and Pro plans, the build is covered by a 12-month minimum term instead of a large one-time bill. If you cancel early, you can buy the site out, and it is then transferred to you.

How long does a new site take?

Most five-page sites launch within three to four weeks of getting your content and photos.

Can you work on my existing website?

Yes. The Essentials plan keeps your current site hosted, secure and updated, and I'll tell you honestly if a rebuild would serve you better.

Can customers book or order online?

Yes. I connect the booking or ordering tool you already use, or help you choose one. Pro includes the full setup.
