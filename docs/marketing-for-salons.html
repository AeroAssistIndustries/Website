Questions

Good to know.

More answers on the FAQ page.

Can you connect our booking system?

Yes. I link the booking tool you already use, such as Square, Vagaro, Boulevard or Fresha, to your Google profile, Instagram and website so clients can book in a tap.

Do clients have to appear in photos?

Only with their permission. Many great salon posts show hands, hair and results without faces.

What about stylists with their own followings?

We can feature each stylist so clients get to know the team, and share posts stylists can repost to their own accounts.
