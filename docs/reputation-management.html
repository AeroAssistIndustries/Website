Questions

Good to know.

More answers on the FAQ page.

Can you remove bad reviews?

Reviews can only be removed by the platform when they break its rules, for example spam or fake reviews. I'll report those for you. For genuine negative reviews, a calm, helpful reply is the best fix, and new positive reviews soon outweigh them.

Do you write fake reviews or offer incentives?

Never. Buying, writing or rewarding reviews breaks Google's policies and federal rules, and can get your profile restricted. Everything I do follows the rules.

Will replies sound like me?

Yes. We agree on your tone and a few phrases you like during setup, and I write every reply in your voice. You can ask to approve replies to critical reviews first.

How fast do you reply?

On Growth and Pro, negative reviews are answered within 24 hours. Essentials covers replies to up to 30 reviews a month, and Growth and Pro reply to every review.

Which platforms do you cover?

Google, Facebook and Yelp on every plan. Pro adds up to three locations or extra platforms like Tripadvisor.
