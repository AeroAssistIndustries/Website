About

Local marketing, done honestly.

Digital Mondo is a one-person marketing studio for locally owned businesses, run by a well-traveled founder who knows how people around the world find the places they love. You work directly with me from your first audit to every monthly report.

Small businesses are great at what they do. They just don't have time to also be marketers. That's where I come in.

Most local business owners know they should post more, ask for reviews and keep their Google profile current. The problem is time. Between serving customers, managing staff and running the business, marketing slips to the bottom of the list.

I take that work off your plate and do it consistently, every month. I focus on the few things that actually bring local customers in, explain everything in plain English, and keep you in control of your accounts and your brand.

My agency is built on a proven model and guided by a mentor with more than 15 years in digital marketing for hotels and local businesses. You get one person's full attention, with an established playbook behind it.

Hi, I'm Karla.

I've traveled widely, and everywhere I go I notice how people find the places they love: the café a local recommends in a group chat, the salon someone saves on Instagram, the hotel a friend shares on TikTok.

Along the way I've used social media the way people around the world actually use it, from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube to Pinterest, LinkedIn, X, Snapchat, WhatsApp, Telegram, WeChat and LINE. That's what lets me help you reach the customers you want, whether they're your neighbors or visitors from the other side of the world.

I started Digital Mondo to bring that perspective home to local businesses: honest, consistent marketing from one person who knows your business and understands your audience.
