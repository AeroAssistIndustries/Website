My commitment

Digital Terra wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, and I bring the same care to the websites and content I create for clients.

What I've done

  • Text and buttons meet color-contrast guidelines, and color is never the only way information is shown.
  • Every page has a "Skip to content" link, a clear heading structure and labeled navigation.
  • All features work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Form fields have labels, and status messages are announced to screen readers.
  • Photos have descriptive alternative text; decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Animations respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Pages work at 200% zoom and on small screens.

Known limitations

  • Some social media example graphics contain text inside images; the same text appears in the caption beside each one.
  • Linked third-party sites and tools, such as Google's PageSpeed Insights, are outside my control and may have their own accessibility issues.

Feedback

If anything on this site is hard to use, I want to know. Email hello@digitalterra.com with the page and the problem, and I'll reply within 2 business days with a fix or another way to get what you need.
