Questions

Common questions.

What does it cost to get started?

Plans start at $250 a month for one service, like reputation management or a website care plan. Most businesses start with one service and add another once they see it working. Two services save 10%, three or more save 15%.

Is it really just you?

Yes. I do the strategy, writing, design and reporting myself. For specialized work like advanced web development or large ad campaigns, I bring in trusted specialists and manage them for you, so you still have one point of contact.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Packages are month to month. I ask for 30 days' notice if you decide to stop, so I can hand everything over cleanly.

How soon will I see results?

Profile fixes and fresh content start working right away, but most local marketing builds over two to three months. I'll set realistic expectations in your audit and show progress in every monthly report.

How much of my time will this take?

About an hour at kickoff, then a few minutes each month to approve your content calendar and send me photos when you have them. I handle the rest.

What if I already have someone posting on social media?

That's fine. I can focus on your Google profile and reviews, or support your team with a content plan and designed posts they can publish.
