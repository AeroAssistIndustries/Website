Why a one-person agency

Most small business owners I talk to have tried marketing help before. Often it went the same way: a big promise, a long contract, a different account manager every few months and a monthly report full of numbers nobody explained.

Digital Mondo is built the other way around. You work with me, the same person every month. I learn your business, your customers and the way you like to talk to them. Plans are month to month, because I'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

A worldly eye for local businesses

I've traveled widely and seen how people in different places discover a café, book a salon or choose a hotel. Some plan everything on Google Maps. Some ask friends in a group chat. Some scroll short videos until something looks good enough to visit. More and more ask an AI assistant.

That's the idea behind our tagline, your neighborhood, seen by the world. Your next customer might live two streets away, or they might be landing at the airport tonight. Either way, they should find you, like what they see and know exactly how to reach you.
