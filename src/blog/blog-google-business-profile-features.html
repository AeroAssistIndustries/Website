Most businesses fill in their name, address and phone number on Google, then never touch the profile again. Here are eight free features that are easy to miss and worth an afternoon.

1. Secondary categories

Your primary category matters most, but you can also add secondary categories for the other things you genuinely do. A bakery that also serves coffee, or a salon that also does nails, can add those. Only add categories that describe your business itself, not every product you sell.

2. Services and products

The services and products sections let you list what you offer, with a short description and, if you like, a price. They give customers a quick menu of what you do, and they help Google understand which searches you're relevant for. Use the words your customers use: "balayage", "AC repair", "gluten-free cakes".

3. Attributes

Attributes are the small labels that appear on your profile, such as wheelchair-accessible entrance, outdoor seating, free Wi-Fi, appointment required or women-owned. The options depend on your category. They answer questions people would otherwise call to ask, and some customers filter by them.

4. More hours

Beyond your regular opening hours, many categories can add separate hours for things like delivery, takeout, brunch, happy hour, senior hours or a drive-through. If part of your business keeps different hours, this stops customers turning up at the wrong time.

5. Special hours

Set special hours ahead of every public holiday, even if you're open as usual. It tells customers you've confirmed the day, and it prevents the frustration of a closed door. It takes two minutes and is one of the easiest ways to keep a profile trustworthy.

6. Booking, menu and order links

Depending on your category, you can add links for appointments, reservations, a menu or online ordering. If you use a booking system, link straight to it so customers can act from the map without hunting through your website.

7. Updates

Updates are short posts with a photo that appear on your profile: an offer, an event, a new product or simply what's fresh this week. A short update every week or two shows you're open and active, and gives people one more reason to choose you.

8. Performance

The performance view shows how people found you and what they did next: calls, direction requests, website clicks and the searches that showed your profile. Check it monthly. It tells you more about real customers than likes ever will.
