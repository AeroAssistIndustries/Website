The holidays are when a lot of new customers find local businesses for the first time. A little preparation helps you catch them, and keep them coming back in January.

On Google and Maps

  • Set special hours for every holiday, including days you're open as normal.
  • Add holiday photos: decorations, seasonal products, gift ideas, your team.
  • Post an update with your holiday offer, event or gift card details.
  • Check your booking, menu and website links still work.

On social media

  • Plan your posts for the season now, so you're not writing them on your busiest days.
  • Share a gift guide: three to five things from your shop at different prices.
  • Post your holiday hours early, then again the week before.
  • Show behind the scenes of the season: wrapping, baking, decorating, prepping.
  • Reply to messages quickly. People shopping for gifts are often deciding that day.

In your shop

  • Promote gift cards at the counter, on your website and on social media. They're an easy last-minute gift.
  • Put a review QR card by the register. Busy weeks bring lots of happy customers. Ask everyone the same way, and don't offer anything in return.
  • Join in with local events, such as Small Business Saturday, the Saturday after Thanksgiving, or a neighborhood market.
  • Collect emails, with permission, for a friendly "thank you and see you in the new year" message.

For visitors in town

Many people travel to see family over the holidays and look for things to do nearby. Make sure your profile has clear photos, accurate hours and a short description that helps someone new understand what you're about. The guide to winning customers who are just visiting has more ideas.

After the holidays

  • Reply to every review that comes in during the season.
  • Thank your customers in a post and share what's coming in the new year.
  • Give gift card buyers and new customers a reason to come back in January.
  • Note what worked so next year's plan is easier.
