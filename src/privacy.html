1. Who this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Mondo LLC ("Digital Mondo", "I", "me") handles personal information collected through this website, by email and while providing marketing services to clients. It applies to visitors, prospective clients and clients.

2. What I collect

Information you give me

  • Audit and contact requests: your name, business name, email, phone number, website, city, type of business and anything you write in your message.
  • Call requests: the topic, day and time you pick, your time zone and the contact details you enter on the booking page.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you sign up for the monthly note on the blog.
  • Client onboarding: billing contact details, business information, brand files and the access you grant to your business accounts.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. I see the payment status and the last four digits of a card, never the full card or bank number.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: the website host records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Third-party content: photos load from Unsplash, which receives your IP address and browser details when your browser requests those images. Fonts are hosted on this website.
  • Website speed check: if you use it, the web address you enter is sent to Google's PageSpeed Insights service to run the test.

3. How I use it

  • To prepare your free audit and reply to your questions.
  • To deliver, bill and report on the services you buy.
  • To send service emails such as reports, invoices and approvals.
  • To keep this website secure and working.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

I don't use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similar effects on you.

4. Who I share it with

I share personal information only with service providers that help me run the business, and only as needed:

  • Email and file storage (Google Workspace)
  • Payments (Stripe)
  • Website hosting and delivery
  • Design, scheduling and productivity tools used to deliver your work
  • Trusted contractors working on your account, who are bound by confidentiality

I may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business. I do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Two features store small amounts of data in your own browser, which never leave your device:

  • The Google Business Profile checklist remembers which items you've ticked (local storage), until you clear it.
  • The plan builder remembers the plan you chose so it can fill in the audit form (session storage), until you close the tab.

If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated first, and any non-essential cookies will be described here.

6. Client account data

When I manage your Google Business Profile, social media, reviews or website, I can see data inside those accounts, including your customers' public reviews, comments and messages. I use it only to deliver your services and don't copy it for any other purpose. You remain responsible for your own privacy notices to your customers.

7. Emails and messages

If you contact me or become a client, I'll email you about your audit or services. I occasionally contact local businesses with a free audit offer; every such email identifies me and includes a simple way to opt out, and I honor opt-outs promptly. If you sign up for the monthly newsletter, I'll send it to the address you give me, and every issue includes a way to unsubscribe. I don't send automated text messages without your consent.

8. How long I keep it

Audit requests from people who don't become clients are kept for up to 24 months. Client records are kept for the length of our work plus up to 7 years, as needed for tax and legal purposes. You can ask me to delete information sooner where the law allows.

9. Security

I use two-factor sign-in on business accounts, manager-level access instead of shared passwords, and reputable providers for email, storage and payments. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects you, as required by law.

10. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop receiving emails, by writing to me. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know what I collect, to delete it, to correct it and to opt out of sales or targeted advertising (which I don't do). I won't treat you differently for using these rights. I'll respond within 45 days and may need to confirm your identity first.

11. Children

This website and my services are for businesses. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent me information, contact me and I'll delete it.

12. Changes

I may update this policy as my services or the law change. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page, and clients will be notified by email.

13. Contact
