About

Local marketing, done honestly.

Karla's Agency is a small, hands-on marketing studio for locally owned businesses. You work directly with Karla, from your first audit to every monthly report.

Small businesses are great at what they do. They just don't have time to also be marketers. That's where we come in.

Most local business owners know they should post more, ask for reviews and keep their Google profile current. The problem is time. Between serving customers, managing staff and running the business, marketing slips to the bottom of the list.

Karla's Agency takes that work off your plate and does it consistently, every month. We focus on the few things that actually bring local customers in, explain everything in plain English, and keep you in control of your accounts and your brand.

The agency is built on a proven model and guided by a mentor with more than 15 years in digital marketing for hotels and local businesses. You get one person's full attention, with an established playbook behind it.
