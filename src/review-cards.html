Paste your Google review link and get a printable counter card or business-size card with a QR code. Customers scan it and land right on your review form. Nothing you type leaves this page.

Where do I find my Google review link?
  1. Sign in to the Google account that manages your business.
  2. Search Google for your business name, or open Google Business Profile.
  3. Choose Ask for reviews (sometimes Get more reviews).
  4. Copy the link and paste it above.

Asking the right way

  • Ask every customer, not only the happy ones. Filtering who gets asked (review gating) breaks Google's rules.
  • Never offer a discount, freebie or entry into a draw for a review.
  • Scan the printed card with your own phone before you put it out.
