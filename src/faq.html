Getting started

What happens after I request a free audit?

I review your Google profile, website, social pages and reviews alongside two or three local competitors, then send a short report with three quick wins, usually within a few business days. If it makes sense, we book a 20-minute call to talk through it.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No cost and no obligation. You keep the report and the quick wins even if we never work together.

Is it really just you?

Yes. I do the strategy, writing, design and reporting myself. For specialized work like advanced web development, I bring in trusted specialists and manage them, so you still have one point of contact.

Which service should I start with?

Most local businesses start with Local SEO or Reputation, because that's where nearby customers find and judge you first. Your audit ends with one clear recommendation.

How soon will I see results?

Profile fixes and new content start working right away. Most local marketing builds over two to three months, and every monthly report shows the progress.

Pricing and billing

How much does it cost?

Each service has three levels: Essentials from $250 a month, Growth and Pro. Social media Pro is the highest at $1,500 a month. See every plan, or build your own, on the pricing page.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Pay for a year upfront and get 12 months for the price of 10 on any plan or combo. Yearly savings stack with combo savings; the founding client rate is for monthly plans.

What is the cheapest way to get all four services?

The All-in-One Starter: social media, your Google profile, review replies and website care in one plan for $595 a month, or about $495 a month paid yearly.

Do you offer discounts?

Two services save 10%, three save 15%, and all four save 20%. My first three clients can choose a founding rate of 15% off for six months instead. Discounts don't stack.

Is there a contract?

Plans are month to month with 30 days' notice. Website Growth and Pro include a new site with no build fee in exchange for a 12-month term.

How do I pay?

A monthly invoice you pay online by card or bank transfer (ACH) through Stripe. Plans are billed in advance.

Is ad spend included?

No. You pay Google or Meta directly from your own ad account, so you always see where every dollar goes.

Services

Which social platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn most often, plus YouTube, Pinterest and others where your customers spend time, including platforms popular with international visitors.

Do you write fake reviews or buy followers?

Never. I help you earn genuine reviews from real customers and grow a real audience. Fake reviews break platform rules and can get your listing penalized.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No one honestly can. I guarantee the work and honest reporting, and I focus on what reliably helps: a complete profile, consistent listings, good pages and steady reviews.

Do you do photography or video?

I plan Reels and shot lists you can film on a phone in minutes, and offer on-site content days as an add-on.

Can you help with ads?

Yes, Google and Meta ad management is available as an add-on, and boosted posts are included in Social Media Pro.
