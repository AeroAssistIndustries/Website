Questions

Common questions.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Packages are month to month. We ask for 30 days' notice if you decide to stop, so we can hand everything over cleanly.

How soon will I see results?

Profile fixes and fresh content start working right away, but most local marketing builds over two to three months. We'll set realistic expectations in your audit and show progress in every monthly report.

How much of my time will this take?

About an hour at kickoff, then a few minutes each month to approve your content calendar and send us photos when you have them. We handle the rest.

Do you work with businesses outside my area?

Yes. Everything we do is online, so we can work with local businesses anywhere in the U.S.

What if I already have someone posting on social media?

That's fine. We can focus on your Google profile and reviews, or support your team with a content plan and designed posts they can publish.
