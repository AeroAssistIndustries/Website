Billing questions

Good to know.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Packages run month to month, and we ask for 30 days' notice before you stop.

Why does each price say “and up”?

Most businesses fit the starting price. Businesses with several locations, more platforms or a lot of reviews may need more work each month, and we'll tell you upfront after the audit.

Is ad spend included?

No. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google or Meta from your own account. Our fee covers managing the ads.

Who owns my accounts and content?

You do. We work inside accounts in your name, and every post, photo and page we create is yours to keep.

Can I change packages?

Yes. You can move up or down at the start of any month.

How do I pay?

We send a monthly invoice you can pay online by card or bank transfer.
