Billing questions

Good to know.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans run month to month with 30 days' notice to stop. The only exception is the Website Growth and Pro plans, which include a new site with no build fee in exchange for a 12-month plan.

Which plan should I start with?

Most local businesses start with Local SEO or Reputation, because that's where nearby customers find and judge you first. Your free audit ends with a clear recommendation and an exact monthly price.

How do bundle savings work?

Choose two services and save 10% on the total, or three or more and save 15%. Founding clients can choose 15% off for their first 6 months instead; the discounts don't stack.

Is ad spend included?

No. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google or Meta from your own account. My fee covers managing the ads.

Who owns my accounts and content?

You do. I work inside accounts in your name, and every post, photo and page I create is yours to keep.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Move up, down or add a service at the start of any month.

How do I pay?

You get a monthly invoice you can pay online by card or bank transfer (ACH).
